Easiest way to get the .jar without installing anything:
1. Create a free GitHub repo and upload everything in this folder (including the .github folder).
2. Open the repo's "Actions" tab -> "Build jar" -> wait ~1 minute.
3. Download MaceProgression-jar from the finished run. Put MaceProgression.jar in your server's plugins folder.

Or locally: install JDK 21 + Maven, then run: mvn package  (jar appears in target/)
